Privacy policy

Your data, in plain words

Last updated 11 November 2026

Lapsy makes routes for walking, running, cycling and more, and guides you along them. This page explains what Lapsy collects, why, and the choices you have. [your name or company] runs Lapsy.

The short version

  • Your health data stays on your phone. Lapsy reads your heart rate from Apple Health during an outing and saves finished outings back to Apple Health. It never uploads Health data.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it from inside the app.

What we collect

Account details. Your email address, and your name if you give one. If you sign in with Apple or Google we receive what they share with us, such as your email and name.

Optional profile details. Weight, height and birth year, only if you enter them. They're used to estimate energy use.

Your outings. Each finished outing is saved to your account: the type of activity, date, time taken, distance, estimated energy, your GPS track, the route you followed, your rating, average heart rate, climb and kilometre splits. Outings under 50 m aren't saved.

Saved routes. Routes you choose to save, so you can run them again on any device.

Shared routes. If you tap Share, the route (its shape, name, distance, climb and where it starts) is uploaded so anyone with the link can see it. Only routes you choose to share are made public.

Your location. Lapsy uses your location while the app is open to find your start point and, during an outing, to track it. Lapsy can keep tracking with the screen locked while an outing is running. Location is used only for these features.

Motion and music. If you allow it, Lapsy reads your step rate from the phone's motion sensor and the song playing in the Music app, to show them on screen. These stay on your phone.

Who else handles data

We use a small number of services to run the app. They process data for us and for no other purpose:

  • Supabase stores accounts, profiles, outings, saved routes and shared routes.
  • OpenRouteService (routing) receives the start point and distance you ask for, to build a route. It does not receive your name or email.
  • OpenStreetMap, via the Overpass service, receives the outline area of a route to look up drinking water and toilets nearby.
  • Apple Maps draws the maps in the app.
  • Apple and Google, if you choose to sign in with them.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the public route pages and this website.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete an outing or saved route, it's removed from your account. When you delete your account, your profile, outings, saved routes and shared routes are deleted from our servers.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Profile, then Delete account.
  • Turn off location, Health, motion or music access any time in iPhone Settings. Parts of Lapsy that need them will stop working.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction by emailing [support email].
  • Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights such as access, correction, deletion and objection. Email us and we'll help.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and each account can only read and change its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Children

Lapsy is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
