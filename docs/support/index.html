Support

Need a hand?

We reply by email, usually within a few days.

Email [support email] and tell us what phone you have, what you were doing, and what went wrong. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

It says routes are busy or can't be made.
Route making depends on an outside service with daily limits. Wait a little and try again.

The map starts in the wrong place.
Allow location for Lapsy in iPhone Settings, Lapsy, Location. You can also tap Change on the Generator screen to pick a start point yourself.

My email confirmation link doesn't work.
If you already confirmed, open Lapsy and tap Sign in. If not, tap Create an account again and we'll send a new link.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and follow the email.

Heart rate doesn't show.
Heart rate comes from Apple Health, for example from an Apple Watch. Allow Lapsy in Health access, and wear your watch during the outing.

My outing wasn't saved.
Outings under 50 m aren't saved. Others are saved on your phone straight away and backed up when you're online.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, tap the profile button at the top right, then Delete account. This removes your profile, outings, saved routes and shared routes from our servers and can't be undone.

How do I stop a shared link working?
Email us the link and we'll remove it.
